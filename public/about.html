About me

A builder’s mindset with a developer’s curiosity.

I’m Damani Howell, a quantity surveying and civil engineering graduate transitioning into tech, combining a builder’s mindset with a developer’s curiosity. With a background in measurement, cost planning, and project delivery from construction, I bring rigorous attention to detail, structured problem‑solving, and clear communication to every codebase. Before learning to code, I worked as a Trainee Estimator and spent several years in logistics and operations, where I learned how to keep complex processes moving, collaborate in teams, and deliver reliably under time pressure—habits I now apply to writing clean, maintainable software.

What I’m learning

Right now, I’m building foundations in programming and web development through hands‑on projects. I focus on translating real‑world problems into simple, testable solutions, documenting assumptions, and iterating quickly—much like preparing a BoQ or a cost plan in NRM2. My current goals are to deepen my front‑end skills, practice version control and collaborative workflows, and ship small, well‑scoped projects that demonstrate clear value and measurable outcomes.

As I grow my developer toolkit, I’ll publish projects that reflect the same discipline used in estimating—clear scope, sensible architecture, thoughtful trade‑offs, and measurable impact. Expect small apps first, then larger case studies with problem statements, tech stacks, challenges, and outcomes. If the work resonates, reach out—collaborations, feedback, and learning opportunities are all welcome.
